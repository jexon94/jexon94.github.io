Peaceful Kadamakkudy Island Living, 100m from the Backwaters

Tucked inside the quiet island village of Valiya Kadamakkudy (also spelled Kadamakudy) just 13 km from Edappally, Joby Homestay is our spacious two-story family villa featuring traditional Mangalore clay-tile accents and breezy verandahs. The property sits in a calm residential lane 100 meters (a 1-minute walk) from the backwaters—there is no direct river view from the house itself, ensuring complete privacy and quiet comfort.

Step 100 meters down the lane to watch migratory egrets gliding over emerald Pokkali paddy fields and Chinese fishing nets—then spend your evenings racing friends on our full PS5 Racing Simulator Rig or enjoying fresh Kerala pearl spot curry cooked right at home.
